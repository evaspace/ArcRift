Compatibility Report

For testing Intel Arc cards (A770, B580, etc.) and headsets (Rift S, Rift CV1). Check my PC is read-only: it changes nothing and starts no session. It writes a compat-report-…txt file in this folder with your GPU, driver, display modes, and Meta runtime lines. Serial numbers are masked.

Before running: keep the headset plugged into the Intel Arc card, Meta Horizon open, and wake the headset briefly.

How to Use

Playing

  • Press Start Meta Dash / Guardian to launch Meta Horizon Link, Oculus Dash, and Guardian directly on your Intel Arc GPU (Steam is not required).
  • Press Start VR session (SteamVR) when you want a SteamVR-only session with Dash disabled.
  • Approve the Windows administrator prompt and leave the PowerShell window open — it manages and verifies full rollback when you stop.
  • When finished, quit your game first, then press Stop & restore and wait for rollback to finish.

Good to know

  • UI Style: Switch anytime between Arc Glass (sidebar layout) and Studio Minimal (top command bar & wide console) using the toggle in the top right.
  • Guardian: Runs natively inside Meta Dash. If you already saved a Guardian, you can jump right into apps or redraw it via Settings > Guardian inside Dash.
  • Taking the headset off pauses the low-fps/freeze guard; putting it back on resumes automatically.
  • Sessions stop automatically on GPU/driver resets, Meta runtime crashes, or low frame rate and restore your display, EDID, and Meta settings.
  • If a session was interrupted (PC restart or closed window), a red banner appears. Keep the headset plugged in and press Recover or start a new session to restore automatically.
  • Each session writes a folder (d3d11-…) with logs and optional PresentMon timings.

Never

  • Never close the administrator PowerShell window or press Ctrl+C during a session — always use Stop & restore.
  • Never run two copies of ArcRift at the same time.
